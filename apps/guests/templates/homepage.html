Before we talk

A few useful answers.

Can we work with you outside the Philippines?

Yes, we welcome international inquiries. We'll discuss remote collaboration, time zones, data-sharing requirements and any local expertise your project needs before agreeing a scope.

Do we need a complete GIS system to start?

No. A conversion task, a dataset needing review or one clearly defined mapping workflow can be a useful starting point.

What should we include in an inquiry?

Tell us your sector, country, source formats, approximate data volume and desired outcome. A description is enough to start; please don't include confidential records in the form.

How are costs and timelines agreed?

They depend on source quality, volume and the work involved. We discuss requirements and agree scope and commercial terms before committing to delivery.
